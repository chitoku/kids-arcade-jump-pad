# Plywood pad — prototype notes

Use a rigid base and top plate with four supported sensing points. Mount each cell according to its manufacturer's intended flexure/load path; leave clearance for the sensing element to deflect. Do not clamp or bridge across the flexure. Route and strain-relieve wires clear of feet, fasteners, and moving gaps. Keep HX711 close to the bridge; twist signal wiring and isolate it from USB/power noise.

Four nominal 50 kg cells do not establish a safe 200 kg jumping capacity: landing forces and off-center loads can greatly exceed static weight and overload one corner. Establish plate strength, stable nonslip footing, guarded edges/gaps and mechanical overload stops before anyone jumps. Stops must protect cells without bypassing them during normal measurement. Start with small static loads and inspect fasteners/deflection before controlled low hops. Firmware is a game input prototype, not a safety or body-weight instrument.

Record here after assembly: plywood dimensions/thickness, cell part number and mounting sketch, stop clearances, unloaded counts, signed counts/kg, corner-load checks, measured SPS and tuned thresholds. These depend on hardware not yet inspected.
